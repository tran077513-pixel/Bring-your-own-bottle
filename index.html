Refill Today. Reduce Tomorrow.

One reusable bottle can replace hundreds of single-use plastic bottles over its lifetime. Let's make that our school's everyday habit, starting with a one-month trial.

See the one-month trialWhy it matters

Small bottles, big pile.

Single-use plastic bottles are easy to grab and just as easy to throw away. At school, that can add up to a steady stream of waste, and most of us rarely stop to think about it.

  • Disposable plastic bottles add to the growing amount of plastic waste.
  • Plastic can stay in the environment for a very long time.
  • Over time, plastic can break into microplastics that may enter ecosystems and food chains.
  • Everyday choices are easy to overlook, so the first step is simply noticing them.

A one-month trial. Nothing more to start.

We are not asking for a permanent change. We are proposing a simple trial to see how a small habit works in practice.

  1. Bring

    Students bring a reusable water bottle to school.

  2. Refill

    They refill it whenever they need water.

  3. Reuse

    The same bottle replaces regularly buying single-use bottles.

  4. Remind

    Simple reminders help everyone keep the habit going.

  5. Review

    We look at participation and, if data is available, compare disposable bottle use before and during the trial.

This is a proposal. It has not yet been approved by the school, and no refill stations have been confirmed.

Two good reasons to try it

Reduce plastic waste

Reusable bottles can replace many single-use bottles over time. The more students take part, the less disposable bottle waste the school may produce.

Build sustainable habits

School is a good place to practise responsible behaviour. Carrying a reusable bottle is a simple daily action that builds awareness of waste and consumption.

"But what if students forget?"

The concernSome students may forget their bottles or find a new routine inconvenient.
Our answerThat is why this starts as a one-month trial, not a permanent rule. Simple reminders help students build the habit step by step.
